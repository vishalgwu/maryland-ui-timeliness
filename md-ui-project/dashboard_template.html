What I would do as lead analyst

  • Measure the right clock. Split delay into agency-caused (adjudication, identity verification, employer response) versus claimant-caused (backdating, late certification). The state says backdating inflates the federal metric; this lets us prove it or fix it.
  • Early-warning model. Forecast weekly initial claims and flag weeks where projected workload will push the 21-day rate below 87%, so staff can be redeployed before a backlog forms.
  • Attack the long tail. About one in five payments is late. A small share of claims with issues (identity checks, separation disputes) likely drives the 29+ day tail; target those queues with a root-cause dashboard.
  • Operational dashboard. Weekly intake, age of pending claims, % paid in 14 / 21 days, by issue type and region, with the 87% target on every chart.
