How employers fund UI

UI is paid for by employer taxes, not by workers.

  • Employers pay into the Unemployment Insurance Trust Fund based on quarterly wage reports.
  • Rate = statewide tax table (A through F, set by Trust Fund balance) combined with the employer's experience rate (benefits paid to former staff versus taxable wages).
  • 2026: Tax Table A, $25.50 to $637.50 per employee on an $8,500 taxable wage base. New employers typically start at 2.3% to 2.6%.
  • Employers who fail to file required reports get the highest rate in the current table.

Overpayment recovery timeline

Click a milestone. Overpayments are benefits paid to people who were not eligible, including through identity theft.

The legal framework

  • Federal law requires recovery, subject to state law.
  • Overpayments must be established within 3 years; those older than 5 years may be deemed uncollectible.
  • Recovered trust-fund money returns to the fund; CARES Act money returns to the federal government. None goes to Maryland's General Fund.

Help for claimants who get a notice

  • Report identity theft to remove the payment from their account.
  • Verify identity, dispute and appeal, request a hardship waiver, or repay.
  • In-person help at headquarters and three American Job Centers; a call-back line within one business day.
