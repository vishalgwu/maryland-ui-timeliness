Key takeaways for the panel

    Methods: data from US DOL ETA 9050 and ETA 539 (public). Rolling rate = sum of payments within 21 days ÷ sum of all first payments over 12 months. Forecast = average weekly pattern since mid-2022 scaled to the last 8 weeks' level; the 80% band uses the residual spread; accuracy is checked with a 13-week backtest.
