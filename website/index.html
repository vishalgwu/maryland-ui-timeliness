      06 · receipts

      How I calculated it (and checked it).

      All public data. All reproducible. Open source.

      The metric

      On-time rate = first payments made within 21 days of the first compensable week ÷ all first payments (intrastate + interstate), from US DOL file ETA 9050. The 12-month rate sums payments first, then divides.

      The sanity check

      The file's delay bands add up to its total, and my annual results match the department's published chart: Maryland 69.3% and US 83.4% for 2025, and the reported 14.1% low in June 2020. I caught and fixed an early column error this way.

      The limits

      The state says backdating inflates the federal metric, and public data can't separate that from agency delay. Correlations are descriptive, the forecast is illustrative, and department figures were read from its slides.
